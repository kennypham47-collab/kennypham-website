About

Built for emotion and impact

Kenny Pham is a melodic bass artist from Salt Lake City, Utah, creating music that fuses uplifting emotion with festival-sized energy. From soaring intros to explosive drops, each record is designed to connect deeply while hitting hard live.

With releases including Don't Let Me Go, Away From Here, and Crawling Out, Kenny continues building a sound that feels cinematic, personal, and powerful.
